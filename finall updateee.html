Free starter kit

CameraYour phone. Turn on the grid lines and shoot horizontal.
EditingDaVinci Resolve or CapCut, both free.
SoundPhone earphones held close to the speaker beat a far-away mic.
WritingFree screenwriting apps like Trelby or Highland's free tier.
LightA window, a white sheet and a lamp. Shoot near sunrise or sunset.
FeedbackPost your film to the crew board below and ask for notes.
